SKIP DAT 1.3
============

Hold Alt and scroll anywhere: a liquid glass wheel of your music queue opens
by the cursor. Let go to play the song in front.

It shows music only: Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, YouTube (music
videos), SoundCloud and other music players.


USING IT
--------
Hold the shortcut and scroll   open the wheel; one notch is one song
Let go                         play the song in front (stay put = no change)
While holding:
  click a song                 play it straight away
  the buttons by the wheel     previous, play/pause, next
  click another app's name     switch to that app's queue
  arrow keys                   move between songs and apps
  Space / Enter                play-pause / play the song in front
  middle click                 play-pause
Esc, or a click elsewhere      close without changing anything

Settings: double-click the Skip Dat icon in the tray, near the clock.
  Shortcut   Alt (default), Ctrl, Shift, Windows key, two-key combinations,
             or the right / Back / Forward mouse button
  Layout     Vertical (default) or Horizontal; applies at once


YOUTUBE MUSIC, YOUTUBE AND SOUNDCLOUD
-------------------------------------
To see their whole queue (mixes, radio and playlists included) and play any
song in it, add the browser companion to Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera once:

  1. Open chrome://extensions  (Edge: edge://extensions)
  2. Turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the "browser-companion" folder
     next to this file. Settings > "Show folder" opens it.
  4. Reload any music tab that was already open.

Settings shows "Connected" once a music tab has talked to Skip Dat.


SPOTIFY'S UPCOMING SONGS (OPTIONAL)
-----------------------------------
Spotify's app doesn't share its queue with Windows. To see it, open Settings
> "Spotify's upcoming songs" and connect a Spotify developer app (Client ID).
Spotify requires the developer app's owner to have Premium.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- While a window running as administrator has focus, Windows doesn't pass
  input to normal apps, so the shortcut does nothing there.
- Exclusive full-screen games draw over everything, including the wheel.
- Firefox isn't supported by the companion yet; its music still shows up,
  without the queue.
- To update, run a newer installer. Your settings are kept.
- To remove: Windows Settings > Apps > Skip Dat > Uninstall. Remove the
  browser companion from your browser too.
